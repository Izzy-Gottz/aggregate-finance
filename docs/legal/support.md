# Support

Email **support@aggregate.finance**. A person reads every message, usually within one business day.

- **Security issues:** security@aggregate.finance (see [Security](/security)).
- **Privacy requests** (a copy of your data, or deleting it): privacy@aggregate.finance, or do it yourself in the app:
  Preferences → Export everything, or Preferences → Delete your Aggregate account.
- **Connecting an AI** (Claude, ChatGPT, Cursor and others): [Connect your AI](/docs/connect-your-ai).

## Common questions

**Can Aggregate move money?** No. It reads your accounts and records what happened. It never pays, transfers or sends
invoices, and no connected AI can either. It emails someone else only when you send an invite.

**How do I stop an AI's access?** In the app, open Connect your AI, choose the AI, and press Pause or Revoke. It takes
effect on its next request.

**A sign-in email opened in the wrong browser.** Type the 6-digit code from the same email on the page where you asked
to sign in.

**How do I delete everything?** Preferences → Delete your Aggregate account. Workspaces where you're the only member are deleted
with all their data.
