# Upload a Lyft, DoorDash or Cash App statement (PDF)

**Short answer:** Lyft, DoorDash and Cash App (personal) don't offer a CSV of your earnings or transactions, only PDF
statements. Upload the PDF in Aggregate (**Workspace → Add anything → Upload a file**). Aggregate keeps it as a
document: nothing in it is added to your totals, so the payouts already in your bank aren't counted twice. Your AI can
read the PDF and answer questions about it, for example your gross earnings, the fees taken, or what the 1099 reports.

If you ask your AI to record what's in it, those amounts would count next to the bank deposits. Ask it to read and
compare, not to add.

## Get the PDF

These are each platform's own steps as their help pages give them. App menus change, so look for the same names if
yours differs.

- **Lyft (drivers):** in the Driver app, open the **Dashboard** menu, choose **Driving history**, pick the week, then
  **Download weekly summary**. Your annual summary and tax documents are under **Tax Center**.
- **DoorDash (Dashers):** in the Dasher app, open **Earnings**, choose **Earnings statements**, pick the month, and save
  the file. Your 1099 is on the same **Earnings** tab.
- **Cash App (personal):** open your profile, then **Documents → Account Statements**, or go to
  [cash.app/account](https://cash.app/account) and open **Documents**. Statements come out within 5 business days of
  the month's end.

## Upload it

1. Open **Workspace → Add anything → Upload a file**.
2. Choose the company or your Personal space.
3. Upload the PDF.

It needs to be a PDF with selectable text. A scan or photo is kept, but your AI can't read its text.

Password-protected PDFs are refused.

Keep each file to 60 pages or fewer.

## Questions to ask your AI

1. *"What were my gross earnings and fees on the Lyft summary I uploaded?"*
2. *"Compare the DoorDash statement for September with the DoorDash deposits in my bank."*
3. *"What does my 1099 report as total earnings?"*

Your AI answers by reading the document. Aggregate doesn't check the figures in it.
