the project can keep using them safely. You keep the copyright in everything you contribute.

There are two ways to sign:

- **Individuals** sign by commenting on their first pull request, as the bot will explain:
  `I have read the CLA Document and I hereby sign the CLA`
- **Companies** whose employees contribute on the company's behalf sign Part B once, by emailing a signed copy to
  support@aggregate.finance with the GitHub usernames it covers. Employees still sign Part A on their first pull request.
