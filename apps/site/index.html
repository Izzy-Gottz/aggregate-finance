    Questions
    Does Aggregate see my bank password?
    No. You sign in to your bank through Plaid, and to each app on its own page. Aggregate gets a token, encrypted with a key kept outside the database, and only ever uses it to read.
    Does my data train AI models?
    No. Aggregate never uses your data to train models and doesn’t let anyone else. What your AI reads is covered by your AI provider’s own policy.
    Can it move money?
    Never. There’s no tool and no connection that can pay, transfer, pay a bill or send anything.
