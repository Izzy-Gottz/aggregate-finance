  • Aggregate can’t move moneyIt only reads your accounts. No tool in Aggregate, for you or for an AI, can move money.
  • You sign in with your bank, not with usBank connections go through Plaid or SimpleFIN, on their screen. We never see your bank password.

For your security review

Company
Aggregate Finance LLC, a New Jersey limited liability company.
Questionnaire
Our CAIQ v4.1 self-assessment is on our CSA STAR listing. These are our own answers, not an audit.
Policies
Eight written policies, approved by the founder on November 1, 2026. Read them.
Subprocessors
Fly.io, Neon, Cloudflare, Resend, Plaid, Vercel and Frankfurter, plus Google or Apple if you sign in with them. What each one handles.
Data flow
Your accounts reach Aggregate through Plaid, SimpleFIN or the provider’s own API. Aggregate sends the AI you connect only what you grant it. Our server doesn’t call an AI model. Data is hosted in the US.
Encryption
HTTPS only. Bank tokens and API keys use AES-256-GCM, each with its own key, under a master key held as a hosting secret outside the database. Backups are encrypted before they leave the server.
Access
Sign-in uses passkeys, a one-time email link or code, Google or Apple, with no passwords. AI access is limited to what you choose and expires after 90 days. Who at Aggregate can see your data.
Incident response
If someone gets into your data without permission, we email the owners of affected workspaces promptly, and as the law requires. Read the policy.
Safe harbor
Good-faith security research is covered by the disclose.io Safe Harbor. Read it.
Not in place yet
No SOC 2 report or other certification, no outside penetration test, no failover outside one US region, no uptime commitment during beta, and no second person reviewing code or access.
Contact
security@aggregate.finance. We aim to reply within 2 business days. For a data processing agreement, write to privacy@aggregate.finance.
