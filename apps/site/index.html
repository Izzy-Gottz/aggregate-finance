What it is

Aggregate is a read-only finance MCP server. It brings your banks, cards, Stripe, QuickBooks, Shopify and payroll into one ledger, then connects it to the AI you already use, such as Claude, ChatGPT or Perplexity.

What you can ask

Cash, burn and runway. Who owes you and what’s due. Why spending changed. A Stripe payout and the bank deposit it becomes count once, so the answers add up.

Your choice

You pick what each AI can see, and every question is logged. Open source.
